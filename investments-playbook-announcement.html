The yield in the listing is not the yield that reaches your account.

Service charge, agency and management fees, void periods, DLD and trustee costs, mortgage payments. Each one is small enough to wave through, and together they routinely take a fifth or more off the gross figure a brochure prints. The site works that arithmetic in the open, every deduction named, so you can put your own building through it.
