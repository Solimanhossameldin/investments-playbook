The numerator drops service charges, management, maintenance, insurance and a vacancy allowance. The denominator adds the transfer fee, agency commission and VAT, trustee and administrative charges, and any mortgage registration fee.

Both adjustments push in the same direction, which is why the net figure sits so far below the gross one.
