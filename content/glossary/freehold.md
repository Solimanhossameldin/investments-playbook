It is the strongest form of ownership: no expiry, no ground rent, and the right to sell, lease or bequeath.

Outside the designated plots, ownership for non-GCC nationals is generally leasehold or restricted. A term is worth less than a perpetuity by an amount that can be computed, which is what makes the distinction a price rather than a label.
