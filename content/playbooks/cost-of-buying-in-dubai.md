On the published schedules, buying a AED 1,500,000 Dubai apartment for cash costs AED 96,220 in fees, which is 6.41% of the price, and AED 103,503 with a 75% mortgage, which is 6.90%. Every line of that is set either by Executive Council Resolution No. (30) of 2013 or by a Dubai Land Department service page, which means it can be added up before an offer rather than quoted as a band afterwards.

And added up, it is not one percentage. It falls as the price rises.

| Price, AED | Fees, cash | As a rate | Fees, with a 75% mortgage | As a rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 400,000 | 27,020 | 6.75% | 32,240 | 8.06% |
| 750,000 | 50,470 | 6.73% | 56,346 | 7.51% |
| 1,500,000 | 96,220 | 6.41% | 103,503 | 6.90% |
| 3,000,000 | 187,720 | 6.26% | 197,815 | 6.59% |
| 5,000,000 | 309,720 | 6.19% | 323,565 | 6.47% |

**Buying a AED 400,000 Dubai apartment with a 75% mortgage costs 8.06% of the price in fees, and buying a AED 5,000,000 one costs 6.47%.** That spread is not a market condition and it is not negotiable. It is what happens when a schedule mixes percentages with flat amounts, and it is the one thing a page that prints "six to seven per cent" cannot tell you.

## The lines, and the instrument behind each one

At AED 1,500,000, bought for cash on the market convention that the buyer carries the whole registration fee.

| Line | AED | Set by |
|---|---|---|
| Land Department registration fee | 60,000 | Resolution 30 of 2013, Schedule |
| Agency commission, with VAT | 31,500 | Market rate, not an instrument |
| Registration trustee, with VAT | 4,200 | Land Department service page |
| Title deed certificate | 250 | Land Department service page |
| Map, apartments and villas | 250 | Land Department service page |
| Knowledge fee | 10 | Land Department service page |
| Innovation fee | 10 | Land Department service page |
| **Total** | **96,220** | |

The registration fee is **4% of the value of the sale contract**. The trustee office is a service partner rather than the Land Department, which is why its fee carries VAT where the registration fee does not: the threshold in the Land Department's own words is **AED 4,000 + VAT If the sale value is AED 500,000 or more** and **AED 2,000 + VAT If the sale value is less than AED 500,000**, and Article 3 of Federal Decree-Law No. (8) of 2017 sets the **standard rate of the tax to be levied on any supply or importation shall be (5%)**. The title deed line is the **AED 250 Title Deed Certificate Issuance Fee**.

One line in that table is not a fee at all. The agency commission is a negotiated rate, shown here at the 2% that is standard in Dubai, and it is the second largest number in the stack. It is also the only one that disappears if nobody acts for you.

## Why the rate falls, which is the part worth knowing

Sort the lines by how they behave rather than by who collects them, and the stack has only two parts.

| Block | Behaves as | At AED 1,500,000 |
|---|---|---|
| Registration fee | 4.00% of price | 60,000 |
| Agency commission with VAT | 2.10% of price | 31,500 |
| Trustee, title deed, map, knowledge, innovation | Flat | 4,720 |

The first two scale, so they are the same percentage at every price. The third does not. On a cash purchase the flat block is **AED 4,720** at or above AED 500,000 and **AED 2,620** below it, where the trustee fee steps down. Register a mortgage and a second flat block of **AED 4,470** arrives with it, because the mortgage has its own trustee at **AED 4,000 service fee**, its own title deed and its own knowledge and innovation fees.

Yes, and the reason is arithmetic rather than policy: a mortgaged purchase at or above AED 500,000 carries a flat block of AED 9,190 that does not change with the price, and that block is 1.23% of a AED 750,000 flat and 0.18% of a AED 5,000,000 one.

Which inverts a piece of advice people give each other. A first, small, mortgaged purchase pays the highest entry rate of any purchase on the schedule, and the AED 400,000 studio that looked like the cautious way in carries 8.06% of fees against the villa's 6.47%. The caution is real and it is not free.

## Who the law makes liable, which is not who pays

Article 3 of Executive Council Resolution No. (30) of 2013 makes the registration fee shared equally by the seller and purchaser in the absence of an agreement to the contrary, and the Land Department's own service page prints it as 2% of the sale value on each side, so a buyer carrying the whole 4% is a term of the sale contract rather than a rule of law.

What that is worth is simply the seller's half.

| Price, AED | Buyer's fees on the convention | If the fee is split as Article 3 splits it | As a rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 400,000 | 27,020 | 19,020 | 4.75% |
| 750,000 | 50,470 | 35,470 | 4.73% |
| 1,500,000 | 96,220 | 66,220 | 4.41% |
| 3,000,000 | 187,720 | 127,720 | 4.26% |
| 5,000,000 | 309,720 | 209,720 | 4.19% |

Two honest qualifications, the same two [net rental yield](/playbooks/net-rental-yield/) makes. The convention is close to universal, the standard Form F allocates the fee, and a buyer who raises this in a hot market will usually be told no. But it is larger than the entire agency commission at every price in the table, and a buyer who does not know the default exists cannot ask for it.

## The mortgage is a second registration, not an extra line

Registering the loan is its own transaction with its own fees. The registration fee is **0.25% of the mortgage (debt) value**, and on a 75% loan against AED 1,500,000, which is AED 1,125,000:

| Line | AED |
|---|---|
| Mortgage registration fee | 2,813 |
| Registration trustee, with VAT | 4,200 |
| Title deed certificate | 250 |
| Knowledge and innovation fees | 20 |
| **Total** | **7,283** |

Note the shape of that. The variable part is AED 2,813 and the flat part is AED 4,470, so on any loan under roughly AED 1,800,000 the paperwork costs more than the fee on the debt. It is also the figure that falls due again on a refinance, because the Land Department prices transferring a mortgage at the rate it prices registering one, which is the arithmetic on [refinancing a Dubai mortgage](/playbooks/refinancing-a-dubai-mortgage/).

What is not here is the bank's own cost of putting the loan in place, and it is absent because it is not capped. The Central Bank's schedule of maximum retail charges sets a ceiling on ten fees on a Dubai home loan and none at all on the arrangement fee charged on the loan or the valuation charged at the bank's rate, which is set out on [mortgage capacity](/playbooks/mortgage-capacity/).

## Off-plan: the same 4%, and a counterparty who might absorb it

The Land Department's initial sale page prints the 4% as two halves, 2% to the seller and 2% to the purchaser, plus AED 10 knowledge and AED 10 innovation fees, and on an initial sale the seller is the developer rather than an individual who will refuse.

That changes what the split is worth. On the resale market the other half belongs to a private seller with no reason to concede it. Off-plan the counterparty is a company running a launch against a sales target, in a market where the fee is routinely absorbed as an incentive, and at AED 1,500,000 it is a AED 30,000 question asked at the point of sale or not at all.

The AED 1,000 [Oqood](/glossary/oqood/) charge that brokers quote to buyers appears on that same page under self-registration fees for developers. It is filed as a developer's cost. The rest of what a payment plan does to the price, and what a default costs, is [off-plan payment plans](/playbooks/off-plan-irr/).

## What is deliberately not in the total

Four things, each left out for a stated reason rather than by omission.

**The developer's no objection certificate.** No instrument sets it, no cap bounds it and no register publishes it. It falls on the seller's side of a resale, which is where [selling well](/playbooks/selling-well/) argues it out, and every figure on this page is a floor because of it.

**The refundable utility deposit.** Money that comes back is a cash flow rather than a cost, and an owner's deposit is returned only on sale. The connection charge itself is small and is priced on the utility's own service page, which [rent or buy](/playbooks/rent-vs-buy/) uses.

**Conveyancing, snagging, furnishing and insurance.** Real, and none of them set by a schedule. A total whose whole claim is that it was added up cannot absorb four numbers that were guessed.

**The service charge.** It is not an entry cost at all. It is the recurring one, it is the largest single determinant of what the property actually yields, and it is the number missing from the listing: [service charges and reserves](/playbooks/service-charge-and-reserves/).

## The question you will ask next

**Is 6.41% a lot?** As a share of price it sounds survivable and as a share of return it is not, which is the only comparison that matters. Set against the rent, the entry stack on this unit is just over a year and a half of net operating income, and the round trip in and back out again is [two point one one years of it](/playbooks/transaction-cost-drag/).

**Is the 4% charged on the price I pay or the price the Land Department thinks it is worth?** On the value of the sale contract, which is the figure registered. A bank's valuation is a separate exercise with separate consequences, covered in [when the valuation comes in low](/playbooks/when-the-valuation-comes-in-low/).

**Does a non-resident pay more?** Not on this schedule. Nothing in the registration fee, the trustee fee or the title deed varies by the buyer's residency, and the place residency does bite is the loan, not the fee, which is [mortgage capacity](/playbooks/mortgage-capacity/).

**What should I actually check before I sign?** The things a schedule cannot tell you: that the seller owns what he is selling, that the service charge is what the budget says, and that nothing is outstanding on the unit. That list is [due diligence before an offer](/playbooks/due-diligence-before-an-offer/).

## Then do the arithmetic the fees are only the denominator of

The whole point of knowing the entry cost exactly is that it goes underneath the rent. The [transfer fee](/glossary/transfer-fee/) is a term; the stack above is a number; what you want is the yield on the number, which is what the [net rental yield calculator](/calculators/net-rental-yield/) computes on your own figures.

Every instrument cited here is listed, with what it sets in its own words and a link to the official text, on the [Dubai property law register](/dubai-property-law/).
